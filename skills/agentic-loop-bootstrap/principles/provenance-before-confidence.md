# Principle: Provenance before confidence

A claim is only as trustworthy as its weakest source. Cross-document repetition from one origin is NOT corroboration. Every emitted file's frontmatter includes a provenance field pointing at the specific answer or spec it came from.

## Where this shows up
- Referenced by [skill.md](../skill.md) as a behavioral rule.
- Enforced by [phase 2 tailor](../phases/02-tailor.md) when emitting files.
- Recorded in every [decisions log](../artifacts/decisions-log.md) entry.
